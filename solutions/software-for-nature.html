01 · Ingesta y limpieza

01

Ingesta y limpieza de datos

Data ingestion and cleaning

Recogemos satélite, LiDAR, clima, catastro y tus propias capas, y los dejamos limpios, alineados y listos para trabajar.

We gather satellite, LiDAR, climate, cadastre and your own layers, and leave them clean, aligned and ready to work with.

02

Modelos y pipelines

Models and pipelines

Procesamiento reproducible en Earth Engine y Python: modelos que clasifican, miden y actualizan solos, sin trabajo manual.

Reproducible processing in Earth Engine and Python: models that classify, measure and update on their own, with no manual work.

03

Visor o API a medida

Bespoke viewer or API

Envolvemos los datos en una interfaz clara o una API limpia, diseñada alrededor de las preguntas que realmente te haces.

We wrap the data in a clear interface or a clean API, designed around the questions you actually ask.

Anyone on the team can query, export and decide on their own. The tool no longer depends on a single person.
